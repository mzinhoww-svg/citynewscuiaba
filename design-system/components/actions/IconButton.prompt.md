Circular 48px icon button for screen headers (back, more, notifications); `badge` adds the Urucum dot.
```jsx
<IconButton icon="arrow-left" label="Voltar" />
<IconButton icon="bell" badge />
<IconButton icon="heart" variant="inverse" size={36} />
```
- `outline` on white screens, `inverse` over photos/Tinta, `ghost` in dense bars.
